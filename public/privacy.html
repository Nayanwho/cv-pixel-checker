Privacy Policy

Effective 30 July 2026

What the service processes

CV Pixel Checker processes text, formatting segments, and layout settings that you or an authorized AI client submit for rendered-width measurement. Do not submit information you do not have permission to process.

How data is used

Submitted content is used only to calculate widths, wrapping, fit status, and related CV diagnostics and to return those results. The application does not require an account and does not sell personal data.

Storage and infrastructure

The application does not intentionally persist submitted CV text in an application database. Hosting and network providers may process transient request metadata and operational logs under their own security and retention practices. ChatGPT or another client may separately retain conversation content according to that client's settings and policies.

Security and limits

Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid submitting secrets, authentication credentials, government identifiers, medical information, or financial account details.

Your choices

You can stop using or disconnect the app at any time. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support page.
